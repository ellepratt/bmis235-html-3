Well Read, Well Said

Welcome readers! Thank you for joining me on this journey to being more well read and well spoken. Throughout my life, I have encountered highs, lows, everything in between. I have deduced, from my extensive life experience, that at the end of the day, all you have is yourself. Thus, in your finite time on earth, it is imperative that you nurture your mind and soul as much as possible. I am dedicating this substack and website to the celebration of this practice. I hope from this that you learn something new and find something you love.

I hope my words inspire you to find yours.

Xo,
