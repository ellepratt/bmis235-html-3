Nightstand Thoughts

We all have those words that race through our minds just as the lights go out. The things we should have said, the things we're afraid to say, the big questions that only seem to show up at 2 a.m. For a long time I let them keep me awake. Now I write them down.

Nightstand Thoughts is where I share them. These pieces are raw, honest, and unpolished by design. They're the thoughts as they arrived, kept close the way a notebook is kept beside the bed.

If you're lying awake tonight with a mind that won't quiet down, I hope you find a little company here. You are not the only one with a racing mind, and sometimes putting the words on a page is the first step toward getting some rest.

Sweet dreams,
Elle
