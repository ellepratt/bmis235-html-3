The Weekly Word

Every week, one idea takes up residence in my mind and refuses to leave. Maybe it's a question I can't answer, a feeling I can't name, or a small moment that turned out to be much bigger than it looked. The Weekly Word is where I finally give that idea the room it deserves.

This is my long form essay, the slow and thoughtful part of the week. I follow one topic wherever it leads, turning it over and looking at it from every angle until I understand it a little better than I did before. I don't always land on a tidy answer, and I've come to believe that's part of the point.

If you've ever needed to write something out to make sense of it, you already know why I do this. I hope these essays give you something to chew on, something to disagree with, or maybe just the words for a thought you've been carrying around yourself.

Happy reading,
Elle
